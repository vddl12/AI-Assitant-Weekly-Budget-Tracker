          About Runway

          Student money doesn’t follow a monthly salary. It arrives weekly, and a printing bill or a surprise dinner can wreck Thursday. Runway tracks your allowance one week at a time and shows how much you can safely spend each remaining day. Advance Allocation lets you reserve money for chosen days ahead of time.

          The Micro-Adjustment Advisor compares your spending to an even pace, warns you early, and rebuilds category limits for the days left. It uses transparent rule-based logic running locally, so it simulates an AI assistant without calling an external model.

          Stack: HTML5, Tailwind CSS (CDN), vanilla JavaScript, browser localStorage. No backend.

          Privacy Policy

          Last updated: October 2026

          What we collect

          Nothing. Runway has no server, accounts, analytics, or advertising. The allowance, expenses, notes and settings you enter are stored only in your browser’s localStorage on this device.

          How the data is used

          Only by the page running in your browser, to show your balance, charts and advisor results. The advisor runs locally, and no expense data is transmitted to us or any third party.

          Third-party resources

          The page loads Tailwind CSS and fonts from public CDNs. Those providers may receive your IP address and browser details when files are requested, as with any website. They never receive your budget data.

          Your control

          You can delete any expense, or erase everything with the button below. Clearing your browser data also removes it. Data is not synced between devices.

          Audience and contact

          Runway is intended for college and university students. Questions can be sent to the course project team listed with the submission.
